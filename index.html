What is PUMPFUN PIRATES?

A free pirate battle game made by and for the PUMPFUN PIRATES community on Solana. Sink enemy ships, grab gold, hire escorts and survive long enough to take on THE COMMODORE.

Do I need crypto to play?

No. Just hit SET SAIL. Connecting a Solana wallet is optional and read-only: it only saves your skins and best run, and it can never move your funds.

What's $AHOY?

The community's token. The game is part of the $AHOY ecosystem: a free side quest for holders and their friends, so you don't need to buy anything to play.
