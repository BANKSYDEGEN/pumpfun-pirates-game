What is PUMPFUN PIRATES?

A free pirate battle game made by and for the PUMPFUN PIRATES community on Solana. Sink enemy ships, grab gold, hire escorts and survive long enough to take on THE COMMODORE.

Do I need crypto to play?

No. Just hit ENTER GAME. Connecting a Solana wallet is optional and read-only: it only saves your progress, and it can never move your funds.

How do I start?

ENTER GAME puts your ship at the harbor dock. Step ashore to walk the island. The plaza boards start STORM HUNT (solo waves) or BATTLE ROYALE (real players, SOLO or SQUADS).

Shops

On the island: CLOTHES SHOP, PET STORE, SHIP SHOP (skins), UPGRADE SHOP, CREW SHOP, plus the SWORD SHOP and MUSKET SHOP in their own buildings up Main Street. Everything is bought with Battle Royale gold and listed cheapest first. The CREW SHOP has a hat colour pick for your whole crew.

SWORD PIT + MUSKET RANGE

On-foot duels: 1v1 free-for-all, or pick RED / BLUE for 2v2 (teammates can't hurt each other). Just for fun: arena KOs never count toward kill goals and wins pay no gold. Swords and muskets raise their own hit rate in on-foot battles. They don't boost cannons.

PRESTIGE

Max every ship upgrade, then hit PRESTIGE in the UPGRADE SHOP. It resets only your ship upgrades; gold, skins, gear, kills and leaderboards stay. PRESTIGE 1 gives the GOLDEN GALLEON free, PRESTIGE 2 the DIAMOND SHIP. Your emblem shows top-left.

Skins you earn

GHOST SHIP unlocks at 100 Battle Royale kills, FLAMING SHIP at 200 (confirmed kills on real players).

Exploring

Swim in the lake, the river, the sea outside the harbor or the SWIM COVE on the west beach. Watch the cliffs: you can fall off ledges. The headland battery's cannons fire for real: they can hit AI and pirate ships out at sea. Treasure maps drop when you sink the Commodore and from Battle Royale kills: follow the X and dig. The radar (top right) shows the island: tap it or press M for the full map.
